
# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: No Way Home

## About the Movie

**Spider-Man: No Way Home** is one of my favorite superhero movies. It follows Peter Parker as he deals with the consequences of revealing his identity to the world.

### Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because of its amazing action scenes, emotional moments, and exciting story. It also teaches the importance of friendship, responsibility, and sacrifice.

## My Favorite Characters

- **Peter Parker** - The main character and Spider-Man.
- **Doctor Strange** - A powerful sorcerer who helps Peter.
- **MJ** - Peter's girlfriend and close friend.
- **Ned Leeds** - Peter's best friend.
